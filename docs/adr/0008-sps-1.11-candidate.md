# ADR 0008: Keep SPS 1.11 / JATS 1.4 as an explicit candidate

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-10-18

## Context

The committed production baselines are SPS 1.9 with JATS 1.1 and SPS 1.10 with
JATS 1.3. A January 2025 Packtools issue records the intended mapping of SPS
1.11 to JATS 1.4, but the public SciELO Publishing Schema documentation found
during the Phase 3 investigation still presents 1.10 as its documented release.

## Decision

Register `SPS-1.11-candidate` with JATS 1.4 metadata, candidate stability, and
external-required assets. Do not register the unqualified `SPS-1.11` identifier,
change project defaults, or claim conformance until normative assets, licensing,
checksums, and acceptance fixtures are available.

Evidence:

- <https://github.com/scieloorg/packtools/issues/814>
- <https://github.com/scieloorg/scielo_publishing_schema/blob/master/docs/source/index.rst>

## Consequences

The architecture can test future-version compatibility without silently changing
production policy. Projects must deliberately opt into the candidate identifier,
and validation remains unavailable until its external assets are installed.
